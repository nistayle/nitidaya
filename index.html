1. Formulasi Fisika Pembangkit (Standar IEC 61724-1)

A. Koreksi Daya Terhadap Temperatur Sel:

P_act = P_stc × (G / 1000) × [1 + γ × (T_cell - 25°C)] × η_inv
T_cell = T_amb + [(NOCT - 20) / 800] × G
γ = -0.38%/°C (Mono-PERC), NOCT = 45°C, η_inv = 97.5%

B. Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE):

LCOE = [CAPEX + ∑(OPEX_t / (1+r)^t)] / ∑[E_gen,t / (1+r)^t]
r = 6.5% discount rate, degradasi sel surya = 0.55%/tahun

2. Pipeline AI Vision & Regresi Hibrida

OCR Parser: Bounding-box detection untuk token IDPEL, TARIF/DAYA, dan KWH berulang.
Inductive Bias: Fitur fisik radiasi surya NASA POWER disuntikkan langsung ke model LightGBM.
Zero Over-Sizing: Membatasi penetrasi daya maksimum sesuai beban dasar siang hari UMKM.
